PART 4 SCHEMA / DATA DICTIONARY

UNSTRUCTURED/documents.csv
document_id, document_type, document_title, case_id, document_date,
source_organization, author_role, language, document_format, document_text,
ocr_required, classification, source_id, confidence_score, created_at

UNSTRUCTURED/observations.csv
observation_id, observation_type, entity_type, entity_id, location_id,
observation_timestamp, description, vehicle_id, source_system,
observer_type, evidence_id, confidence_score, created_at

EVIDENCE/evidence.csv
evidence_id, evidence_type, source_id, source_document_id, source_record_id,
description, collection_timestamp, event_timestamp, location_id,
reliability_score, extraction_confidence, verification_status,
verified_by, verification_timestamp, hash, created_at

EVIDENCE/sources.csv
source_id, source_type, source_name, source_system, source_organization,
collection_method, reliability_score, access_level, data_classification,
created_at, updated_at

ENTITY_RESOLUTION/aliases.csv
alias_id, entity_type, entity_id, alias_value, alias_type,
source_id, confidence_score, created_at

ENTITY_RESOLUTION/entity_resolution.csv
resolution_id, record_1_type, record_1_id, record_2_type, record_2_id,
similarity_score, matching_features, resolution_result, resolution_method,
confidence_score, verified, created_at

ENTITY RESOLUTION LABELS
SAME_ENTITY      = candidate alias/record matches canonical entity
DIFFERENT_ENTITY = candidate alias/record does not match canonical entity
UNCERTAIN        = insufficient/conflicting information; requires review

EVIDENCE PRINCIPLE
Every evidence row should be traceable to a source and, where applicable,
to a document or structured source record. Evidence is provenance, not proof of guilt.
